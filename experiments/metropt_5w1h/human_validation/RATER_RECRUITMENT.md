# 评分者招募文案(可直接转发)

## 中文版

我们在做一项关于"AI 生成设备维护报告的可信度"的研究,需要招募评分者
对一批维护报告进行盲评。要求:

- 具备工程背景(机械/机电/工业工程优先,熟悉设备维护更佳);
- **不是**本项目作者;
- 约 45–60 分钟,一次或分两次完成均可;
- 全程在本地网页上操作,答案以文件形式下载提交,匿名代号即可。

任务:阅读 30 段设备遥测摘要,对每段的四份 AI 维护报告打分
(忠实性/完备性/连贯性/可行动性等 1–5 分)+ 可读性排名。
无需任何编程或 AI 知识,有简短说明页。

参与完全自愿,可随时中止;数据仅用于学术研究,不收集个人身份信息。
如愿意参与,请回复获取评分表单文件(rating_form.html)。

## English version

We are studying the trustworthiness of AI-generated equipment maintenance
reports and recruiting raters for a blind evaluation. Requirements:

- Engineering background (mechanical / mechatronics / industrial
  engineering preferred; familiarity with equipment maintenance a plus);
- NOT an author of this study;
- ~45-60 minutes, in one or two sittings;
- Fully offline: a local web page; answers are downloaded as a file and
  returned, identified only by a self-chosen code.

Task: read 30 telemetry summaries and rate four AI-generated maintenance
reports each (faithfulness, completeness, coherence, actionability, etc.,
1-5) plus a readability ranking. No programming or AI knowledge needed.

Participation is voluntary and can be stopped at any time; data are used
for academic research only; no personal identifying information is
collected.
